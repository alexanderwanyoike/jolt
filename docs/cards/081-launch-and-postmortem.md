# 081: Launch and Postmortem

**Type:** HITL  
**Milestone:** v0 Endgame  
**Status:** Ready after 080
**Blocked by:** 080

## Why

The point of v0 is to learn whether Jolt has legs. After freeze, the project
should be shown to people and judged honestly.

## What to Do

- Publish a concise write-up explaining what Jolt is and is not.
- Share Pastey and Spoke as PoCs.
- Explain the core idea: local apps can use user-owned identity, scoped
  authority, private sharing, optional availability, and recipient-controlled
  communication.
- Ask for concrete feedback from developers and potential users.
- Decide whether to continue, pause, or bin the project.

## Success Signals

- People understand the product bet without a long protocol explanation.
- Someone wants to try Spoke or Pastey.
- A developer can imagine building an app on Jolt.
- Feedback points to a sharper next product path.

## Failure Signals

- Users still see no reason to use Jolt over Tailscale plus normal apps.
- The setup burden overwhelms the value.
- Two-way communication and identity concepts remain too abstract.
- Interest is only in the protocol, not in using or building with it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Medium/HN-style post drafted.
- [ ] Setup/demo links are included.
- [ ] Known limitations are stated honestly.
- [ ] Continue/pause/bin decision is recorded.

## Notes

If the project sprouts no legs, archive the learning clearly and stop.
